About this app

DBT Pocket Coach is an educational tool for learning and practising skills inspired by Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT). It is not a therapist, crisis service, diagnostic tool or replacement for individualised professional care.

Skills can affect people differently. You can stop or choose a different exercise at any time. You do not have to push through something that feels uncomfortable or unsafe.

Privacy by design

This version does not have accounts, analytics or a server. Your favourites are stored in this browser's local storage. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see them. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Do not enter names, identifying information or confidential health details into this app. No journal entries are saved or sent anywhere.

About the content

These original educational summaries are inspired by established DBT concepts. They are not a complete DBT treatment programme. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any DBT training organisation.
